Frequently Asked Questions

Quick details about launching and using our AI chatbots.

Do I need an account to try these AI chatbots?
No DiginixIT account is required to launch any assistant in the directory. Several tools—including Chat Assistant, Product & Market Research, Laws & Regulations, and the DiginixIT Knowledge Assistant—work with instant access, while Science Research, Medical Research, Business Research, and Language Tutor accept a Groq API key directly inside their workspace.
Which chatbot should I choose first?
Pick Chat Assistant for general conversational tasks, Laws & Regulations or Product & Market Research for cited web comparisons, and DiginixIT Knowledge Assistant when looking for information about DiginixIT capabilities. You can also launch Toolkit Hub to switch between tools from one central dashboard.
Can DiginixIT build a custom AI assistant for our organization?
Yes. Alongside these public productivity tools, our engineering team builds custom semantic search assistants, internal knowledge bots, and compliant workflow integrations tailored to your private documentation. Reach out through our contact page to scope a build.
