Does Webware.ai Have a Blog? Corporate Blog vs. AI Blogging Tool

DN
Dr. Nadeem Nazir
2026-11-18 • ~5 min read

If you are wondering does webware.ai have a blog, the short answer is yes in two distinct ways. At Diginixit, we help business owners evaluate digital marketing platforms and choose the right technology for sustainable growth. First, Webware.ai publishes its own educational corporate blog featuring advice on digital marketing, local search, and company news. Second, Webware.ai includes an automated blogging tool inside its all-in-one software platform, using its Rivi Intelligence assistant to generate articles for small business websites. However, many business owners find the difference between their corporate publication and their client blogging tool confusing. This guide breaks down both sides of the platform, explaining how the software works, what it costs, and key limitations you should know before signing up.

Does Webware.ai Have a Blog for Small Businesses?

When prospective buyers research Webware.ai, they usually have two different questions in mind. Some want to read marketing insights published by Webware's team. Others want to know if Webware's website builder includes a native blogging engine to help them rank on search engines.

Webware satisfies both needs, but the execution differs significantly depending on whether you are reading their content or using their software, as detailed in the overview table below.

Inside Webware.ai's Official Corporate Blog

Webware.ai maintains an active first-party blog organized across multiple archive pages. Their articles focus on practical growth tactics for local service businesses, covering topics like pay-per-click advertising, social media setup, and generative engine optimization.

The company also uses its blog to share company announcements, such as partnerships with Bell Canada's Business Resource Hub and Staples. While their content covers helpful marketing topics, the public index displays a few formatting quirks. Article cards on the main blog feed do not show publication dates or category tags, though dates appear once you click into individual articles.

How the Rivi AI Blogging Tool Works for Client Websites

Beyond publishing its own articles, Webware sells automated blogging as a core software feature. Housed within their "Attract" product suite, the tool uses an artificial intelligence assistant called Rivi to research, draft, and publish articles directly to your business website.

The system operates through a straightforward setup process:

  • Business Intelligence Training: You enter target services, geographic service areas, and customer profiles into your Business Intelligence settings page.
  • Automated Topic Generation: Rivi scans your target keywords and suggests relevant article topics designed to attract local search queries.
  • Drafting and Formatting: The software writes the article, generates relevant digital images, and auto-populates meta titles and descriptions.
  • Multi-Channel Distribution: Through the Webware Automation Engine, completed posts can automatically push to linked social media accounts.

Modern businesses often connect these content tools with specialized AI business automation agents and automated social media marketing management to expand their audience reach without spending hours writing drafts manually.

Key Workflow Limitations: The Pre-Publish Approval Gap

While automated publishing sounds convenient, buyers should understand a major operational limitation before turning on autopilot. According to official Webware Help Center guides, articles created through the fully automated scheduler cannot be held for human review before they go live on your website.

If you want to review and edit content before publication, you must avoid the hands-off automation engine. Instead, you have to use their manual three-step workflow: generate the draft, review the copy by hand, and click publish yourself. For businesses with strict branding guidelines, reviewing drafts manually is essential to prevent factual mistakes.

Additionally, customizing website layouts and blog templates on Webware requires knowledge of the Liquid templating language. If your team lacks coding experience, making deep design changes can prove difficult without relying on custom web engineering services.

Webware.ai handles pricing differently than typical website builders. While standard platforms like WordPress, Wix, and Squarespace publish transparent monthly tiers, Webware hides pricing for its "Attract" suite behind a mandatory sales demonstration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Webware.ai have a blog where I can submit guest posts?

No. Webware.ai does not offer a public guest posting program or editorial submission page. Their corporate blog is written exclusively by internal staff writers and executive contributors.

Does Webware.ai automatically optimize blog posts for SEO?

Yes. The Rivi blogging tool automatically adds title tags, meta descriptions, and image alt text based on your business keywords. You can learn more about search optimization on the Wikipedia SEO overview.

Can I edit an AI-generated blog post before it goes live?

Yes, but only if you generate the post manually. If you use their automated scheduling engine, posts publish directly to your live website without a pre-publish review step.
